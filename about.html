MyJournal+

Our Story

A better place to reflect.

MyJournal+ is a calm, private space designed to help you reflect consistently. Since its first release, the app has matured from a simple journal into a lightweight but powerful personal reflection platform.


Your entries and mood records remain private to your account unless you explicitly export or share them.

Privacy

Private by default.

Journaling is personal. MyJournal+ keeps your content private to your account and only uses third-party services where necessary for authentication, storage, AI processing and analytics.


We minimize what is shared and avoid sending identifiable content to third parties without your explicit consent. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
